## Material differences

The table above is generated on every run. The site-by-site account of where this output differs from a human audit, and which result the evidence supports, is kept by hand in [material-differences.md](material-differences.md), because a verdict on evidence is a judgement and should not be regenerated silently.

Differences that hold on every site, because the pipeline does something the human reports do not:

- **Per-parcel dispositions.** The human reports give one site-level recommendation and mark exclusions only as a colour on the parcel map. Every parcel here carries Pursue / Do not pursue / Unresolved with a reason code, so the comparison has no human column to check against.
- **Every parcel in the ring.** The human parcel lists stop at the parcels worth describing; ours lists every parcel intersecting the ring, which is why our counts are higher on every site.
- **The fall-distance setback.** Sec. 8.004.220.12 sets the setback at the manufacturer's designed fall distance, which does not exist at desktop stage. The default assumes full tower height, which is conservative and excludes parcels a human analyst might carry forward pending a fall-zone letter. `setbacks.fall_distance_ratio` controls it.
- **Dwelling distance applied geometrically.** Sec. 8.004.220.13 is enforced as keep-out circles of tower height around detected dwellings. The human reports note the rule but do not measure it per parcel.
- **Tower counts by status.** The FCC file holds granted, cancelled, dismantled and terminated registrations beside constructed ones. Ours counts constructed structures as towers and lists the rest separately; the 2024 reports give one number for 'registered towers'.
- **Two level scales.** The assignment puts a conditional use permit at level 4; the 43986 and 37954 audits put a typical CUP at level 3. Both are printed per jurisdiction, so compare audit scale with audit scale.
