DISTRICT USE PROVISIONS
=======================

M-1 INDUSTRIAL DISTRICT - Uses Permitted by Right

35. Telephone and other communication services, including satellite stations and
towers.

C-1 GENERAL COMMERCIAL DISTRICT - Uses Permitted by Right

52. Telephone and communication services, except satellite stations and towers.

CBD CENTRAL BUSINESS DISTRICT

Incorporates the uses permitted in the C-1 district, subject to listed
exceptions. Telephone and communication services are not among the stated
exceptions. Whether the C-1 carve-out of "satellite stations and towers" carries
across is not stated in the ordinance.


SPECIAL USES
============

Section 5(A), item 7:

Radio tower or broadcasting station.

Special uses are granted by the Mayor and Council, following review by the
Planning and Zoning Commission.

Section 5: Any proposed special use shall otherwise comply with all the
regulations set forth in the ordinance

Section 5(B): In considering any application for a special use permit, the
Planning and Zoning Commission and City Council [make findings]. A commercial or
industrial special use in a residential district shall not be approved unless it
is determined that the proposed use would be of limited duration or compatible.

Section 5(C): Special uses shall commence within one year from the date of
approval, unless extended by action of the City Council.

Section 5(D): Revocation proceedings may be initiated by a majority vote of the
City Council or the Planning and Zoning Commission.


HEIGHT AND AREA REGULATIONS
===========================

Section 6, Height:

The height regulations prescribed herein shall not apply to chimneys, cupolas,
tanks, church spires, belfries, domes, monuments, fire and hose towers,
observation towers, transmission towers, flagpoles, radio and television towers,
masts, aerials, cooling towers, water towers, elevator shafts, windmills,
conveyors, and other similar projections.


DISTRICTS PRESENT IN THE ORDINANCE
==================================
AG (agricultural), R-1, R-1M, R-2, R-3, C-1, CBD, M-1, F.

The ordinance uses "special use", not "conditional use". There is no A-1 or A-2
district in the City of Oakland; those are Pottawattamie County districts.
